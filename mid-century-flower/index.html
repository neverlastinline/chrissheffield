The Idea

Flowers, reduced to their happiest shapes.

Mid Century Flower is a love letter to the 1950s and '60s — the era when a daisy became a circle ringed by ovals, and that was somehow more flower than any photograph.

Every piece here is drawn with nothing but vectors: solid fields of warm colour, confident black outlines, and the kind of symmetry that makes a room feel calm. No stock images, no filters, no AI smudge — just shapes arranged with intent.
