    How to play

    1. Build a deck of at least 5 AFL player cards. Each card has 5 stats — kicks, handballs, marks, tackles, goals — rated 0–100.
    2. Tap Play. Your top card faces up. The CPU's is face-down.
    3. Pick a stat. CPU card flips. Highest value wins the round. Ties don't score.
    4. Next card, repeat. When either deck empties, the higher score wins the match.

    Your deck and history are stored in your browser only — clear your data and they're gone. No accounts, no servers.
